Map display data downloads straight to the head unit. Routing and search come from a USB stick with a HEADUNIT_MAPS folder (made on your computer with maps/build-routing.py). It imports by itself when you plug the stick in.

Updates download in the background whenever the head unit is on Wi-Fi (your home network or your phone's hotspot). Only updates signed with your release key are accepted. A downloaded update installs at the next key-on, before the screen starts; if it doesn't start properly, the head unit goes back to the previous version by itself.
